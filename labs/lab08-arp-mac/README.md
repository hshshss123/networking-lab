# Lab 08 — ARP & MAC

## Objective
Understand how IPv4 devices discover the MAC address associated with a local IP address.

## Concepts
- MAC address
- ARP request
- ARP reply
- IP-to-MAC mapping
- Local broadcast
- Neighbor information

## Exercises
1. Identify what information is available about local neighbors on your authorized network.
2. Observe ARP/neighbor information where Android permits it.
3. Relate an IP address to a local link-layer address.
4. Explain why ARP is normally relevant only to the local network segment.

## Android Note
Unrooted Android can restrict access to low-level neighbor and ARP information. Record limitations rather than treating missing output as proof that no neighbors exist.

## Safety
Perform discovery only on networks you own or are explicitly authorized to test.

## Questions
- Why does a device need a MAC address on a local Ethernet/Wi-Fi network?
- Why does a router separate local and remote networks?
- What is the difference between an IP address and a MAC address?
