# Lab 05 — TCP, UDP & Ports

## Objective
Understand how applications use transport protocols and ports.

## Concepts
- TCP
- UDP
- Source port
- Destination port
- Listening service
- Connection
- Reliability and ordering

## Exercises
1. Identify services on your own device or authorized lab host.
2. Observe TCP connections made by a simple client.
3. Compare TCP and UDP conceptually.
4. Record the local and remote ports when available.
5. Explain why an open port alone is not evidence of a vulnerability.

## Safety
Only inspect or test devices and services you own or have explicit permission to test.

## Questions
- Why do ports exist?
- When is TCP useful?
- Why might an application choose UDP?
- What is the difference between a listening port and an established connection?
